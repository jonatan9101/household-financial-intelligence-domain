# ADR-002 — Capabilities over Epics

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-08-01

## Context

Epics are temporary planning artifacts.

## Decision

Organize the Blueprint around business capabilities.

## Alternatives Considered

- Technology First
- Data Model First
- Domain First (Selected)

## Consequences

### Positive

- Stable roadmap
- Domain-oriented evolution

### Negative

- Product team must separate planning from domain
