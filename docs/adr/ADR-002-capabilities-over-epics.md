# ADR-004 — Separate Facts and Interpretations

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-08-01

## Context

Facts and their meaning evolve differently.

## Decision

Model facts independently from classifications and interpretations.

## Alternatives Considered

- Technology First
- Data Model First
- Domain First (Selected)

## Consequences

### Positive

- AI-ready
- Explainability
- Preserved evidence

### Negative

- Additional bounded context
