# ADR-009 — Business Before Technology

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-08-01

## Context

Technology changes faster than the business.

## Decision

Business terminology and rules never depend on frameworks or cloud providers.

## Alternatives Considered

- Tighter runtime coupling
- Shared database
- Selected approach

## Consequences

### Positive

- Long-term maintainability
- Easier migrations

### Negative

- Requires discipline during implementation
