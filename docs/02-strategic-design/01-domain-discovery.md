# Domain Discovery

## Purpose

Describe how the HFI domain was discovered and refined.

## Business Problem

Households receive financial information from multiple fragmented sources.
The challenge is not collecting more data, but transforming financial facts into actionable knowledge.

## Discovery Outcomes

- Capabilities identified instead of epics.
- Facts and interpretations are modeled separately.
- Small Aggregates.
- Technology-independent domain.
- Future Domain documented explicitly.
