# Domain Introduction

---

## Purpose

This document introduces the **Household Financial Intelligence (HFI)** domain.

It explains the business problem the platform addresses, defines the domain boundaries and establishes the conceptual foundation for every decision described throughout the Domain Blueprint.

This document intentionally avoids implementation details, technologies and architectural patterns.

Its only purpose is to explain **the domain**.

---

# Problem Statement

Managing household finances is still a fragmented and largely manual activity.

Families typically receive financial information from multiple independent sources:

* Bank statements
* Credit card notifications
* Email receipts
* Digital invoices
* Payment confirmations
* Mobile banking notifications

Although these sources contain valuable financial information, they rarely provide meaningful knowledge.

Most personal finance applications require users to manually:

* Register expenses
* Categorize transactions
* Build budgets
* Maintain recurring payments
* Analyze spending

This manual effort introduces friction, reduces adoption and often causes financial records to become incomplete or outdated.

As a consequence, families lose visibility of:

* where money goes,
* how spending evolves,
* which expenses are avoidable,
* and whether financial goals are achievable.

---

# Opportunity

The information required to understand household finances already exists.

The challenge is not collecting more information.

The challenge is transforming fragmented financial facts into reliable financial knowledge.

HFI addresses this challenge by modeling the financial domain before implementing any technology.

---

# Domain Scope

The HFI domain is responsible for representing and understanding the financial reality of a household.

Its responsibilities include:

* acquiring financial information,
* preserving financial facts,
* interpreting financial movements,
* generating financial knowledge,
* assisting financial decision making.

The domain does **not** replace banking systems.

It does **not** execute financial transactions.

It does **not** manage investments or accounting.

Its purpose is to build an intelligent financial model for households.

---

# Target Users

The initial version of HFI focuses on traditional households.

A household is composed of:

* one administrator,
* one or more members,
* shared financial goals,
* shared financial responsibilities.

Future versions may support more complex family structures without modifying the core domain.

---

# Business Objective

The primary objective of HFI is not expense tracking.

The primary objective is enabling better financial decisions.

Expense tracking is only one capability required to achieve that objective.

# Domain Boundaries

The HFI domain starts when financial information becomes available.

Examples include:

* a payment confirmation email,
* a credit card notification,
* a bank statement,
* an invoice,
* a digital receipt.

The HFI domain ends when financial knowledge has been generated and made available for decision making.

Activities outside this boundary belong to external systems.

---

# Domain Philosophy

The domain is based on three fundamental concepts.

## Facts

Facts represent events that occurred in the real world.

Facts are immutable.

Examples:

* Financial Movement
* Financial Account
* Financial Document

---

## Interpretations

Interpretations provide meaning to financial facts.

Interpretations may evolve over time.

Examples:

* Category assignment
* Merchant normalization
* Spending purpose

---

## Knowledge

Knowledge is produced from facts and interpretations.

Examples:

* Monthly spending
* Budget execution
* Financial health
* Spending trends

Knowledge is always derived.

It is never considered the source of truth.

---

# Domain Vision

The long-term vision of HFI is to evolve from a financial recording platform into a trusted financial copilot capable of helping households make informed financial decisions.

The roadmap towards that vision is intentionally incremental.

Each capability builds upon previously established knowledge.
