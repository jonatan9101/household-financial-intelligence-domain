# Business Rules

## Financial Facts

- BR-001 Financial facts are immutable.
- BR-002 Every movement belongs to one household.
- BR-003 Every movement belongs to one financial account.
- BR-004 Original evidence is always preserved.

## Interpretation

- BR-005 Categories may change without changing the fact.
- BR-006 Knowledge is derived from facts.

## Processing

- BR-007 Processing is idempotent.
- BR-008 Duplicate documents never create duplicate movements.

## MVP

- BR-009 MVP targets traditional households.
- BR-010 Future concepts remain in the Future Domain.
